This is certainly not a new invention of any sorts, but a couple of months ago I discovered Michael Chu's recipe format, ["Cooking for Engineers"](https://www.cookingforengineers.com/), that simplifies recipes down to a table based on ingredients and when you use them. LOVED IT! It's been on the mind since.

Additionally, this year I ended up making the Butternut Squash Pasta by the Wishbone Kitchen multiple times for friendsgivings / personal meal prep. However, when I first had the idea this year I discovered that the original recipe seems to no longer be on the website. Maybe it's now paywalled...? I had to piece it together from some tiktok videos, other blog posts, and trial and error.

I decided that, since I needed to write the recipe down anyways, I would try and combine the recipe and the Cooking for Engineers format:
